An Arena in which to Act

At a certain moment the canvas began to appear to one American painter after another as an arena in which to act, rather than as a space in which to reproduce, re-design, analyze or “express” an object, actual or imagined. Harold Rosenberg, “The American Action Painters”, ARTnews, December 1952

    Each room is a working simulation of one way of making a picture between 1943 and 1970. The paint has weight, the canvas drinks, the colour hums. When something on the canvas holds you, press Hang and it joins your exhibition with a museum label. Sound matters; headphones help. A mouse is best, touch works.

    Your Exhibition

    Everything you hung, with its label. Labels are written from what happened on the canvas: how long you worked, how far the paint travelled, how the picture measures. The reviews are imagined. Long-press or right-click a picture to save it.
