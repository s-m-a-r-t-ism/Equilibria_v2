Long Delayed Echoes

In December 1927 an engineer in Oslo listened to the shortwave station PCJJ in Eindhoven and heard its signals arrive, and then arrive again, about three seconds later. Echoes like these are still reported and still not fully explained.

This is a song for someone who sends into the dark every night and hears their own voice come back changed.
